The sushi map: twenty restaurants in Niigata City

The twenty restaurants from our sushi feature, plotted. Fifteen are in and around Furumachi, the old geisha and merchant quarter; three sit by Niigata Station; one is in Nuttari, the old market district; and one is inside the Pier Bandai food market. Click a pin for prices and details. Most pins are on the restaurant's exact address; a few are placed at street or district level.
